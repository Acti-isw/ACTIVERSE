Golden Seams privacy policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Golden Seams (in Spanish, Vetas de Oro) is an Android game by ACTI Studio, part of ACTI (actisw.com). This policy explains what information is used when you play and why.

1. What the game stores on your device

Your progress (repaired vessels, times, Daily piece streak), your hints and your settings are stored only on your device. You don't need an account, and we don't receive this information. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows Google AdMob ads: an optional video to earn hints, and ads between vessels. To show them, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, Google's ads SDK may automatically collect:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate the device's approximate location;
  • the Android advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • interactions with the app and the ads (app launches, taps, video views);
  • diagnostic information (launch time, hangs, energy usage).

This data is encrypted in transit and handled by Google under its policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice in the game's settings. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, and manage your ads at Google My Ad Center.

3. Purchases

Golden Seams Plus is purchased through Google Play. Google processes the payment, and we don't receive your payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing results

The Share button opens the Android share menu with a text (for example, your Daily piece result) that you choose where to send. The game doesn't send anything on its own.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Your rights

Because we don't store your personal data on our own servers, we have no data of yours to access, correct or delete. For data Google processes for advertising, use the controls described in section 2. For any question or request about your privacy, contact us.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with its update date.
